Getting started

What is Menufi?

Menufi plans your meals and workouts around your menstrual cycle. It tracks your cycle, works out your calories and protein, and suggests food and exercise that suit the phase you’re in.

Do I need an account?

No. Everything works on your phone without one. Signing in with your email backs up your plan, logs and cycle history, so nothing is lost if you change phones.

I didn’t get my sign-in code

Check your spam or promotions folder, and wait a minute before asking for a new code. If it still doesn’t arrive, email us from the address you’re signing in with.

Using the app

Plan, Log or Guide: which should I pick?

  • Plan follows your meal plan, one meal at a time.
  • Log lets you search any food and add it as you eat.
  • Guide gives you targets for each meal, with nothing to log.

You can change it any time in Account → Nutrition plan. What you’ve already logged stays.

A food’s numbers look wrong

Open the food and tap Edit nutrition to correct it. Your fix is saved to your foods. For packs, you can also scan the barcode or take a photo of the nutrition label.

How do I connect Apple Health?

Go to Fitness → Sync Apple Health and allow access. Menufi reads your weight, period days and workouts, and saves the food you log. You can change this any time in the iPhone Settings app → Health → Data Access & Devices → Menufi.

Can I see the intro again?

Yes: Account → See the Intro Again. Your answers are filled in, so you can change just what you need.

Your data

How do I delete my account?

In the app, go to Account → Delete account. This removes your account and the data saved to it. To clear everything on your phone too, use Account → Erase everything.

Where is my data stored?

On your phone, and in your account only if you sign in. Read the full privacy policy.

Health

Menufi offers general guidance about food and exercise. It isn’t medical advice, and it can’t diagnose anything. Please talk to your doctor before making big changes to your diet or training, especially if you’re pregnant, have a medical condition, or have had an eating disorder.
