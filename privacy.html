Menu-fi Privacy Policy

Effective 14 November 2026

The short version
  • Your cycle, health, weight and food data stay on your phone unless you turn on account backup.
  • We don’t sell your data, show ads, or track you across other apps.
  • Usage stats are off unless you say yes, and never include health, cycle or food details.
  • Photos of food labels are read on your phone and never uploaded.
  • You can delete your account and its data from inside the app at any time.

Who we are

Menu-fi is a cycle-aware nutrition and fitness app. In this policy, “we” means the Menu-fi team. You can reach us at ashimasinghh@gmail.com.

What stays on your phone

By default, everything you enter is stored only on your device. That includes your profile (name, birthday, height, weight, goals, diet, photo), period dates and cycle logs, symptoms and energy, meals, workouts and challenges.

Account and backup (optional)

If you create an account (sign-in is a code sent to your email), we store:

  • Your email address.
  • A backup of your app data listed above, so it stays in step across your devices.

This is held by our database provider, Supabase. Row-level security means only you can read or change your backup. Your optional Claude API key is never included in the backup.

Apple Health (optional)

If you connect Apple Health, Menu-fi reads your weight, period days and workouts, and saves the calories and nutrients you log. Health data is used only inside the app to tailor your plan. It is never used for advertising or shared with anyone, and it is not included in analytics.

Camera and photos

Menu-fi uses your camera to scan barcodes on food packs and to photograph nutrition labels. You can also choose a photo you already took. Label photos are read on your phone, then discarded. They are not uploaded or kept.

Looking up packaged food

When a pack isn’t already on your phone, Menu-fi sends its barcode, or the product’s name, to find its nutrition:

  • Open Food Facts, an open food database.
  • UPCitemdb, a barcode lookup service.

Nothing about you is sent with these lookups.

Shared pack database

When you’re signed in and save a pack’s nutrition from its label, the barcode, product name and nutrition values are added to Menu-fi’s shared pack database, so other people who scan that pack get the numbers. If you report a shared pack’s numbers as wrong, that report is counted.

Your account is linked to what you added or reported, privately, only so that each person counts once. Other users can’t see who added or reported anything. These links are deleted when you delete your account. Product information you added (barcode, name, nutrition) may remain in the shared database, since it isn’t about you.

Meal assistant (optional)

If you add your own Claude API key in Profile, the meal text you type into the assistant is sent to Anthropic to estimate nutrition. The key stays on your device. Without a key, the assistant works entirely on your phone.

Usage stats (optional)

On first launch, Menu-fi asks whether you’d like to share anonymous usage stats. If you say yes, we send a short list of events to PostHog (EU servers), such as “a tab was opened”, “a meal was logged” or “a barcode scan found a match”. These events never contain your health, cycle, weight or food details, food names, barcodes, or your identity. Session recording and automatic tracking are off. You can change your answer any time.

Reminders and widget

Reminders are scheduled on your phone. The home and lock screen widget reads your upcoming cycle days, meals and workout from your phone only.

What we don’t do

  • We don’t sell or rent your data.
  • We don’t show ads or use advertising identifiers.
  • We don’t track you across other apps or websites.

Deleting your data

  • On your phone: Profile → Erase Profile, or delete the app.
  • Your account: Profile → Account → Delete Account. This permanently removes your account, your backup and your links to anything you added to the shared pack database.

Food data credits

Nutrition data comes from USDA FoodData Central (public domain), IFCT 2017 by the National Institute of Nutrition (India), Open Food Facts (Open Database License), labels read by Menu-fi users, and Menu-fi’s own home-recipe estimates. All numbers are estimates.

Not medical advice

Menu-fi offers general guidance about food and exercise. It isn’t medical advice. Please talk to your doctor before making big changes to your diet.

Changes

If this policy changes, we’ll update the date at the top and, for important changes, tell you in the app.
